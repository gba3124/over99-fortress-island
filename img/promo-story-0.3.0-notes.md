# OVER 99 Fortress Island: story promo notes

## Alt text

- **Hero:** Four blocky players defend a striped lighthouse on a grassy fortress island while fish in sneakers rush the beach. Chunky gun, Tesla and harpoon turrets fire across the walls; a vast glowing eye rises from the stormy sea and fireworks burst overhead.
- **Rising:** After the final boss, four players watch from a wooden dock as the sea splits and a cliff-ring island rises around a red-and-cream lighthouse and stone compass plaza.
- **Boss:** At night, four players fight a molten lobster boss beside the lighthouse. Toy-like turrets fire, sneaker-wearing fish swarm the plaza, and one player crouches to repair a wall.

## Short captions

1. The sea has one more surprise.
2. Four friends. One lighthouse. Far too many sneakers.
3. Every tenth wave, the boss comes back worse.

## Core story

Beat the final boss and the furious sea raises a fortress island around a lighthouse and its pearl. Bring three friends, build walls and turrets, and hold the beaches against 100 waves of fish in sneakers. Every tenth wave brings a boss with a second form; survive wave 100 to go **OVER 99**.
